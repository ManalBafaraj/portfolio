Technology logo sources
=======================

Most SVG marks in this folder are unmodified files from Simple Icons v16:
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

The CSS3 and Java marks are unmodified SVGs from Devicon:
https://github.com/devicons/devicon

The marks identify technologies listed in the portfolio. Product names and
marks remain the property of their respective owners; their use does not imply
endorsement. Icons are displayed at a consistent size without recoloring or
distortion. Refer to each brand's current guidelines where applicable.
